Yurt: A collaborative Platform for Students

Introduction

Yurt is an all-in-one collaborative platform designed to simplify academic life for students. With features like real-time chat, an interactive whiteboard, AI-powered assistance, and secure file sharing, Yurt addresses the challenges of juggling multiple apps for educational needs. By creating a focused and distraction-free environment, Yurt empowers students to collaborate effectively and enhance their academic productivity.

Key Features

Real-Time Chat

Engage in seamless communication with peers through text-based and voice chat options, ensuring collaboration without delays.

Interactive Whiteboard

Brainstorm and solve problems visually in real-time, making group studies more engaging and productive.

AI-Powered Chatbot

Get instant study support with Yurt’s intelligent chatbot, designed to answer academic questions and provide personalized recommendations.

Customizable Servers and Spaces

Create and join topic-specific servers and spaces tailored to your academic interests.

Secure File Sharing

Easily share and organize academic materials with a structured file directory system linked to study servers.
